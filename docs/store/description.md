# Store page text

## Short description (Steam limit: 300 characters)
The night of the flood, a paper fox ran and left his friend to drown. Now the ink is rising through the pages of a waterlogged storybook. Take up Gran's stork shears, cut your way through six ruined regions, and face what you left behind.

## About this game
**Patch and the Pop-Up Kingdom** is a dark, hand-painted action-adventure set inside a storybook that never dried out.

Patch is a fox folded from paper and stitched together with red thread. On the night of the flood he ran, and left Drizzle, the little rain cloud who trusted him, to drown. Since then the Sogg, a mountain of pulped pages and black ink, has been rising through the book. Every page it touches goes soft and wrong.

**Fight like a tailor.** Gran's antique stork shears work three ways. Closed, they're the Bill. Split apart, they're twin Wings. Wide open, they're the Beak. Parry with a crease, dodge edge-on to the blow, and unstitch staggered enemies with brutal finishers. A Thread meter lets you mend your own tears in the middle of a fight.

**Six drowned regions, one Spine.** Wade through the flooded meadows of Thistledown and the lightless harbour of Lanternfold, then climb on to Watermark Marsh, the frozen Spine Peaks, a Gatefold Fair that never closed, and the Index, where everything is listed and some things are being erased. Every region is sewn to the Spine, a hub where Gran's sewing room still keeps its lamp lit.

**Bosses that remember.** The Old Millwheel, the Blotch Kraken, the Grey Heron, Old Bindwell, the Barker and the Redactor each guard a region. Below them all, in the Gutter, the Sogg waits.

**Fold the world.** Pop-Up Slam, Frog Fold, Paper Plane Glide, Blot Dive and Bookmark Warp open new routes through pages you thought you knew.

**After the end.** Rematch every boss on Gran's proof press for gold stickers, take on the Combat Lab trials, play the penny theatre, find the hidden true ending in the Last Margin, then start a Second Printing in heavier type.

- A full-length story, plus a post-game with rematches, trials and a true ending
- Every story line fully voiced
- Controller and keyboard/mouse
- Assist mode and accessibility options. Nobody is keeping score.

## Content notice (for the Steam questionnaire)
Rated 16+. Fantasy violence against paper creatures (tearing, cutting, ink in place of blood). Themes of abandonment, guilt, grief and despair. Dark horror imagery. No sexual content, no real-world violence, no gambling with real money (the penny theatre uses in-game scraps).
